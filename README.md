## 🚀 About Me

I'm a developer focused on **Java and Spring back-end development**, currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science.

I've been building projects since 2021, and back-end is where I found my main interest: **turning business rules into organized, reliable, and maintainable systems**.

I'm also the **founder of [DuckCo](https://duckco.dev.br)**, a software company that builds websites, landing pages, and custom systems for businesses. Before writing any code, we listen to the business first, so you talk to the people who understand your problem and the people who build the solution.

On a daily basis, I study and apply **Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, SOLID, TDD, Clean Architecture, DDD, and microservices**.

🎯 My goal is to grow as a software engineer and, in the long term, work with **software architecture and solution design**.

💼 I'm open to opportunities where I can contribute, learn, and work with people who value **code quality, best practices, and continuous improvement**.
