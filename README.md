## 🚀 About Me

I'm a developer focused on **Java and Spring back-end development**, currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science.

I've been building projects and exploring different areas of software development since 2021, but back-end development is where I found my main interest: **understanding how to transform business rules into organized, reliable, and maintainable systems**.

On a daily basis, I study and apply concepts such as **Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, SOLID, TDD, Clean Architecture, DDD, and microservices**.

🎯 My goal is to grow as a software engineer and, in the long term, work with **software architecture and solution design**.

💼 I'm open to opportunities where I can contribute, learn, and work with people who value **code quality, best practices, and continuous improvement**.
